## Why this offer works (internal, 30 seconds)

*   Dream outcome: senior projects get done and security holds, without a hire.
*   Likelihood proof: 20+ certified engineers, help desk through CISO level. Real numbers: device delivery cut from weeks to days, about 2.5 hours of IT labor saved per endpoint per year, next-business-day replacement.
*   Time delay: credit lands at signing. Assessment findings reviewed within 10 business days.
*   Effort: co-managed by design. We plug into their team; nothing gets ripped out.
*   Risk reversal: the credit plus the dated findings review. No invented deadlines on the credit. If asked when it ends, the honest answer is "it is current now, and I will tell you before anything changes."

*   No: thank them, mark closed-nurture, quarterly list. A clean no is a good outcome.
*   Any reply at all: log it in the CRM within 24 hours and pull them out of the remaining touches.
