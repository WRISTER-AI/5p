# South Shore Drop-Off Target List

In-person prospecting universe for 5 Point Technology, v3 (updated 2026-08-25):
217 drop-off targets across 15 drivable half-day routes covering the full 30 to
45 minute ring around Scituate and the Rockland office, plus the full 49-account
book bucketed for in-person visits, plus a drop-off verdict on every company
from the prior 2026 targeting exercise.

## Files

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `rockland-hanover-drop-off-targets.xlsx` | The working spreadsheet: Read Me, Target List (filterable, with visit-tracking columns and account flags), Existing Accounts in-person plan, Prior 100 Verdicts, Routes, Drop-Off Playbook |
| `data/targets.csv` | Flat export of the 217 targets for CRM import |
| `data/weekly-gameplan-targets.csv` | Slim 15-column export used for the Notion weekly gameplan and the Google Drive sheet |
| `data/accounts.csv` | The 49 existing accounts with drive zone and suggested action |
| `data/prior-target-verdicts.csv` | Drop-off verdict for each of the prior 100 targets |

## What's in v3 (beyond the 111-target v2)

Five new routes, 106 new targets, extending the map from the Rockland/Hanover
core to the whole ring reachable in 30 to 45 minutes:

- **S1 Weymouth / Hingham / Cohasset / Scituate** (21 stops, 10 tier A): the
  commute route. South Shore Bank, Coastal Heritage Bank, Hingham Institution
  for Savings, Electroswitch, Healthcare South, AJA International.
- **Q1 Quincy / Milton** (19 stops): Crown Colony and Hancock St office
  clusters. Bay State Milling, Quincy Mutual, XS Brokers, Twin Rivers.
- **P2 Plymouth / Kingston / Duxbury / Marshfield** (18 stops): home field.
  Engine Lease Finance is in 5PT's own building at 225 Water St.
- **K1 Brockton / Easton / Bridgewater / Avon / Randolph** (26 stops): the
  densest manufacturing on the list; Manley St, Norfolk Ave and Bodwell St are
  three- and four-stop streets.
- **F1 Foxboro / Mansfield / Norton / Sharon / Walpole** (22 stops): Forbes
  Blvd spine. **Competitor note: Thrive, a ~1,400-person national MSP, is
  headquartered in Foxborough** — expect incumbents on this route; lead
  co-managed.

The expansion was screened from ~1,400 raw companies in those towns; MSPs and
IT providers were excluded as competitors, not targets. A handful of
misclassified ZoomInfo contacts (non-IT roles tagged as IT) were removed.

## Sources

ZoomInfo company + contact data (2026-08-24/25), `Chris_Accounts__Targets.xlsx`,
and the Google Drive files `5 Point 2026 Targets Master` and

## Caveats

- Employee counts are estimates and often span all locations of a company.
- Contact names age quickly; confirm at the front desk.
- The Parent/ownership column flags subsidiaries where IT decisions may be made
  elsewhere; the Acct status column flags rows with existing 5PT history.
