# Rockland & Hanover Drop-Off Target List

In-person prospecting universe for 5 Point Technology: 104 businesses within about
15 minutes of Rockland and Hanover, MA (Rockland, Hanover, Norwell, Pembroke,
Whitman, Abington, Hanson, plus one flagged nearby HQ), sized for the 5PT ICP and
grouped into drivable half-day routes for decision-maker drop-off visits.

## Files

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `rockland-hanover-drop-off-targets.xlsx` | The working spreadsheet: Read Me, filterable Target List (with visit-tracking columns), Routes, and a Drop-Off Playbook |
| `data/targets.csv` | Flat export of the same 104 targets for CRM import |

## How the list was built

- Company universe: ZoomInfo company search across zips 02370, 02339, 02061,
  02351, 02382, 02341, 02359 at 20+ employees (268 raw results), pulled 2026-08-24.
- Curated down to 104: excluded town government, public schools, police,
  restaurants/salons, other IT providers and MSPs, and defunct or misgeocoded
  listings.
- Each keeper enriched via ZoomInfo for street address, phone, size, revenue,
  founding year, and parent ownership; flagged rows spot-verified on the web.
- Decision-maker names merged from ZoomInfo contact search (IT department and
  C-level passes).

## Tiers

- **A (29)**: bullseye ICP fit. 50 to 500 staff or compliance-heavy vertical
  (healthcare, finance, manufacturing), decision maker on site.
- **B (45)**: strong fit. Typically 25 to 100 staff with a real office and
  operations.
- **C (30)**: opportunistic. Branches of larger companies, large local anchors,
  trades and dealers. Visit when already on the route.

## Caveats

- Employee counts are ZoomInfo estimates and often span all locations of a
  company, not just the local address.
- Contact names age quickly. Confirm at the front desk.
- The Parent/ownership column flags subsidiaries and acquisitions where IT
  decisions may be made elsewhere.
