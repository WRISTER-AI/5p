# Rockland & Hanover Drop-Off Target List

In-person prospecting universe for 5 Point Technology, v2 (updated 2026-08-25):
111 drop-off targets across 10 drivable half-day routes on the South Shore of
Massachusetts, plus the full 49-account book bucketed for in-person visits, plus
a drop-off verdict on every company from the prior 2026 targeting exercise.

## Files

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `rockland-hanover-drop-off-targets.xlsx` | The working spreadsheet: Read Me, Target List (filterable, with visit-tracking columns and account flags), Existing Accounts in-person plan, Prior 100 Verdicts, Routes, Drop-Off Playbook |
| `data/targets.csv` | Flat export of the 111 targets for CRM import |
| `data/accounts.csv` | The 49 existing accounts with drive zone and suggested action |
| `data/prior-target-verdicts.csv` | Drop-off verdict for each of the prior 100 targets |

## What's in v2 (beyond the original 104)

- **Account flags.** P.A. Landers is an active account, Clean Harbors (Safety-Kleen's
  parent) is a current account, and UPPAbaby also sits on the 2026 target list.
  Those rows are flagged so nobody cold-pitches an existing relationship.
- **Route B1** (Braintree, Holbrook, Canton, Stoughton, Norwood): seven new targets
  seeded from the June `5Point_Postcard_List_FINAL`, several with named IT
  decision makers and emails on file (Boston Mutual's CISO, Abington Bank's IT VP).
- **Existing Accounts tab**: all 49 accounts located and bucketed. Six are Zone 1
  (bookable this month), including Linchris Hotel Group, which shares 5PT's own
  building at 225 Water St in Plymouth.
- **Prior 100 Verdicts tab**: 5 real drop-off candidates (UPPAbaby, Russelectric,
  Globe Composite, IDRx, Manet Community Health), 5 maybes, and honest "No"
  verdicts (badge-access metro / too far / out of region) with the right next
  move for each.

## Sources

ZoomInfo company + contact data (2026-08-24/25), `Chris_Accounts__Targets.xlsx`,
and the Google Drive files `5 Point 2026 Targets Master` and
`5Point_Postcard_List_FINAL`, spot-verified on the web. Several postcard rows had
scrambled geography (a Holbrook NY firm listed as local; Beam's office is
Cambridge, not Quincy; Resolute's office is Boston, not Quincy) and were
corrected or dropped.

## Caveats

- Employee counts are estimates and often span all locations of a company.
- Contact names age quickly; confirm at the front desk.
- The Parent/ownership column flags subsidiaries where IT decisions may be made
  elsewhere; the Acct status column flags rows with existing 5PT history.
